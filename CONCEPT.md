# Hallway of the Dead (working title)

## The game in its barest form

Strip everything away and here's what's left: you stand in a hallway, grey
blocky zombies walk at you, and you swing a sword to cut them down. A ring
pulses around each one and your swing only lands if it's timed with the
pulse. That's the whole game. No score screen, no hearts, no zombie types,
no music sync yet — just walk, wait, swing, hit. If that core loop isn't
fun on its own, nothing we layer on top will save it.

## The idea

You're stuck at the end of a long hallway with a sword, and zombies keep
shuffling toward you. There's music playing, and the zombies move to it —
when the melody changes, they charge.

Here's the twist: every zombie has a ring around it that pulses along with
the beat. Your slash only counts if you hit the zombie right as the ring
closes in. So the song isn't just vibes — it's literally telling you when
to swing. Get the timing right and you slice them down. Miss it and your
blade bounces off.

Kill zombies on the beat to build up a score multiplier. Let one reach you
and you lose a heart. Three hearts, then you're done.

The whole thing should take about 30 seconds to understand: see ring close,
swing sword. Getting *good* at it is where the fun is.

## How it plays

- Swing the sword. That's it, that's the controls.
- Ring around each zombie shrinks on every beat. Slash when it lines up.
- Perfect timing = kill or big damage. Slightly off = half damage.
  Really off = bounce off and your blade locks for a second.
- Some zombies are just tougher and need more clean hits. No special
  combos or patterns to remember.
- Zombies stand there until the melody shifts, then they come at you.
  The music warns you before it happens.
- 3 hearts. Zombie touches you, you lose one.
- Perfect kills in a row bump your multiplier. Take a hit, it resets.

One rule to learn, everything else comes from listening to the track and
reacting fast.
